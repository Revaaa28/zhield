Shielded or Exposed?

Zcash gives you a choice: every payment can be private, or public forever. Most people never check which one they are about to make. Zhield checks for you — before you share a payment request, and after a payment lands on chain.

Transparent payment
Sender, recipient and amount are visible to anyone, forever. Like posting your bank statement.
Shielded payment
Sender, recipient and amount are hidden by zero-knowledge proofs. Only the fee and the timing are public.

Everything on this page runs in your browser. No keys, no accounts, nothing is sent anywhere except the public explorer lookup you start yourself.

4 · The four ways ZEC can move

Every Zcash payment is one of these. The difference is entirely in which addresses it touches — and it is decided by the wallets involved, often without the user ever being told.

t → t · Transparent

Privacy 0/100

Both sides use transparent addresses. Sender, recipient and amount are public — exactly like Bitcoin.

t → z · Shielding

Privacy 55/100

Money enters the shielded pool. The sender and the amount going in are visible; the recipient and everything after are hidden.

z → t · Deshielding

Privacy 45/100

Money leaves the pool. The recipient and the amount coming out are visible; the sender and everything before are hidden.

z → z · Fully shielded

Privacy 100/100

Both sides shielded. Sender, recipient, amount and memo are hidden. Only the fee, block and timestamp are public.

Three things most guides get wrong in 2026

  • Ironwood is the current shielded pool. It activated with NU6.3 on 28 July 2026 and succeeded Orchard, after a soundness flaw was found in Orchard. Current wallets put an Ironwood receiver first in a Unified Address.
  • A Unified Address is not automatically private. It is a bundle of receivers (ZIP-316) and can include a transparent one. Good wallets prefer shielded receivers — but "u1" alone is a default, not a guarantee.
  • A memo is not a note field. It is an encrypted 512-byte message that only shielded payments can carry, readable only by the recipient. A payment link that attaches a memo to a transparent address is invalid by spec (ZIP-321).
